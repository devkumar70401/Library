## 2. The 3-Hour Body Training Architecture

> **Endocrine Note:** Intense lifting is capped at 75 minutes in the evening to prevent **cortisol spike-induced testosterone suppression**. The remaining time is allocated to circadian light mobility and scrotal cooling active recovery.

```
┌───────────────────────────────┬───────────────────────────────┬───────────────────────────────┐
│ 07:30 AM – 08:30 AM (60 min)   │ 05:30 PM – 06:45 PM (75 min)   │ 09:45 PM – 10:30 PM (45 min)   │
│ Morning Light & Core Mobility │ Main Calisthenics & Arm Hyper  │ De-Stress Walk & Scrotal Cool │
└───────────────────────────────┴───────────────────────────────┴───────────────────────────────┘
```

### A. Session 1: Sunrise Circadian Walk & Mobility (07:30 AM – 08:30 AM)
* **20 Min Gentle Walk (Sunrise Window):** Direct exposure during **UV Index 0 to 1** (zero tanning, zero solar urticaria/pitthi, stimulates retinal melanopsin for melatonin timing).
* **40 Min Core & Posture:** Deadbugs (3x15), Bird-dogs (3x12), Planks (3x60s), Cat-cows, and thoracic spine openers to reverse desk-slouching.

### B. Session 2: Main Upper-Body & Thick-Arm Hypertrophy (05:30 PM – 06:45 PM)
*Perform 4 days per week (e.g., Mon, Tue, Thu, Fri)*

1. **Standard / Elevated Push-ups:** 4 sets × 12–15 reps (Pectorals, Anterior Deltoid)
2. **Inverted Table Rows or Pull-ups:** 4 sets × 8–10 reps (Latissimus, Upper Back, Bicep Flexion)
3. **Diamond / Chair Dips:** 3 sets × 12 reps (Triceps Lateral & Long Heads)
4. **🔥 Dedicated "Thick Wrist & Forearm" Circuit:**
   * **Towel / Doorframe Dead Hangs:** 3 sets × 45–60s (Forces deep finger flexors and wrist tendon sheaths to thicken).
   * **Heavy Backpack Hammer Curls (Books inside):** 3 sets × 12–15 reps (Directly targets the **Brachioradialis** for upper-forearm width).
   * **Reverse Wrist Curls (Palms down over knees):** 3 sets × 15 reps (Forearm extensor thickness).
   * **Fingertip Floor Planks:** 3 sets × 30s (Tendon and grip density).

### C. Session 3: Evening De-stress Walk & Scrotal Cooling (09:45 PM – 10:30 PM)
* 30 min relaxed walk in loose cotton clothing (brings scrotal temperature down to $2^\circ\text{C} - 3^\circ\text{C}$ below core temperature).
* 15 min hip-flexor and hamstring static stretches to decompress the lumbar spine after prolonged sitting.
